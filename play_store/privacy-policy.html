StudySeco Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

StudySeco ("we", "us") is a Malawian secondary education platform operated by Emphx Innovative Solutions. This policy explains what information the StudySeco mobile app and website (studyseco.com) collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored in encrypted form), school, and class form (Form 1–4).
  • Profile photo: if you choose to upload one.
  • Learning activity: subjects you enrol in, lessons completed, quiz results and progress, and posts or comments you make in the community area.
  • Payment information: the amount, payment reference and payment status for subscriptions. Card and mobile money payments are processed by our payment provider (Paystack); we do not store your full card details. If you pay manually, we collect the proof-of-payment image you upload.
  • Technical information: basic device and connection information needed to keep you signed in and deliver content securely.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To provide lessons, study materials, quizzes and track your progress.
  • To process and verify subscription payments.
  • To run the community features and moderate content.
  • To contact you about your account, payments or important service updates.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run StudySeco (for example Paystack for payments and our hosting provider), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. Teachers on the platform may see the progress of students enrolled in their subjects. Posts you make in the community are visible to other users.

4. Data security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and sign-in tokens are kept in secure storage on your device. We keep your information while your account is active and delete or anonymise it when you delete your account, except records we must keep for legal or accounting purposes (such as payment records).

5. Children

StudySeco is built for secondary school students. If you are under 13, a parent or guardian should create or approve your account. We collect only the information needed to provide the learning service and do not show targeted advertising in the app.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your StudySeco account and the associated data at any time:

  1. Email support@studyseco.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my StudySeco account".
  2. We will confirm the request and delete your account, profile, progress and community posts within 30 days.

Payment records may be kept for up to 7 years where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

You can view and update your profile in the app, and ask us for a copy of your data or to correct or delete it by contacting us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date.
