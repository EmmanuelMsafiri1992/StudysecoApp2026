**Full description:**
StudySeco is Malawi's secondary education platform, built for Forms 1 to 4 and MSCE preparation.

Learn at your own pace with lessons from experienced Malawian teachers, check your understanding with quizzes, and track your progress subject by subject.

WHAT YOU GET
• Video and text lessons for Mathematics, English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and more
• Lesson transcripts you can read along with or revise from later
• Quizzes that show how well you understand each topic
• A library of study materials shared by teachers
• Progress tracking and achievements so you know what to revise next
• A community where students can ask questions and help each other

BUILT FOR MALAWIAN STUDENTS
Content follows the Malawi secondary school curriculum, so what you study in the app matches what you learn in class and what comes up in exams.

Start preparing smarter for your exams with StudySeco.
